# InventorySaaS — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## What We Collect
- Your Square merchant ID and business name
- Your Square catalog items and inventory counts
- Waste events you log (item, quantity, reason, cost)
- Recipes and ingredients you create
- Your email address (from Square OAuth)

## What We DON'T Collect
- Your Square password (we use OAuth, we never see it)
- Customer data from Square
- Payment card details (Stripe handles all billing)
- Order history or transaction data

## How We Use Your Data
- To display your inventory levels
- To calculate waste costs and generate reports
- To sync data between Square and our service
- To manage your subscription

## Data Storage & Security
- Square access tokens are encrypted with AES-256 at rest
- All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS
- Webhook endpoints verify HMAC-SHA256 signatures
- Our database is hosted on [PROVIDER] with encryption at rest

## Data Deletion
You can disconnect your Square account at any time from Settings. This deletes all your data from our servers within 30 days. To request immediate deletion, email support@inventorysaas.com.

## Third-Party Services
- **Square** — for POS, catalog, and inventory data
- **Stripe** — for subscription billing
- We do not sell, share, or monetize your data

Email: support@inventorysaas.com
