# InventorySaaS — Onboarding Guide

Welcome! This guide walks you through getting started with InventorySaaS.

## 1. CONNECT SQUARE (30 seconds)

1. Go to [APP_URL]
2. Click "Connect Your Square Account"
3. Sign in with your Square credentials
4. Review and approve the permissions we request:
   - **Inventory Read** — to see your stock levels
   - **Items Read** — to see your catalog
   - **Orders Read** — to track sales (future COGS feature)
   - **Merchant Profile Read** — to know your business name and locations
5. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

> **We never get your Square password.** Authentication is handled entirely by Square's secure OAuth flow.

## 2. FIRST SYNC

1. Click "Inventory" in the sidebar
2. Click "Sync from Square"
3. Your entire Square catalog loads with current stock levels
4. Items are color-coded: green (in stock), amber (running low), red (out of stock)

This sync takes 10-30 seconds depending on catalog size. After the initial sync, inventory updates automatically via Square webhooks — no manual refreshes needed.

## 3. START TRACKING WASTE

Go to Waste in the sidebar and log your first waste event:

1. Type the item name
2. Enter the quantity wasted
3. Select a reason (spoilage, overprep, dropped, expired, trim, other)
4. Optionally enter cost per unit for accurate dollar tracking
5. Click "Log Waste"

The event appears in your waste log instantly. Over time, the Reports page will show you patterns — what gets wasted most, what it costs, and where to focus.

**Tip:** Log waste as it happens. A 5-second entry during service beats trying to remember everything at end of shift.

## 4. BUILD YOUR FIRST RECIPE

Go to Recipes and create your first recipe:

1. Enter the recipe name and selling price
2. Click the recipe to open detail view
3. Add each ingredient with name, quantity, unit, and cost
4. The plate cost and margin calculate automatically

Build your top 10-20 dishes first. Once your recipes are in, you'll know exactly what each plate costs and which menu items are actually profitable.

## 5. RUN YOUR FIRST REPORT

Go to Reports, select a period, and run. You'll see:

- **Total waste cost** — the dollar amount going in the bin
- **By reason breakdown** — is it spoilage? Overprepping? Dropped plates?
- **Top wasted items** — which ingredients hurt the most

Run this weekly. The goal isn't perfection — it's improvement. Each week, pick one item from the top 10 and figure out why it's being wasted.

## TIPS FOR RESTAURANT OWNERS

1. **Log waste during service, not after.** A 5-second log saves 5 minutes of reconstruction later.
2. **Start with your 10 most expensive proteins.** Track those first — they hit margins hardest.
3. **Recipe cost anything you sell more than 20 of per day.** The top items drive the numbers.
4. **Run waste reports every Monday.** It takes 2 minutes and keeps you honest.
5. **The first month is about baselining.** Don't try to fix everything at once — first, just measure.
