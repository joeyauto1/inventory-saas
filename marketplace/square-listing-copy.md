## FULL DESCRIPTION

### What It Does
InventorySaaS gives independent restaurants a simple, affordable way to manage inventory and track food waste — right from their Square account. No spreadsheets, no guesswork, no $200/month price tag.

After connecting your Square account (30 seconds), your entire catalog and inventory syncs automatically. From there, you can:

- **See current stock levels** for every item in your Square catalog, with low-stock and out-of-stock alerts
- **Log waste in seconds** — select an item, enter quantity, pick a reason (spoilage, overprep, dropped, expired, trim, other), and we calculate the cost
- **Build recipes with costs** — map your Square catalog items as ingredients, set selling prices, and see real-time plate costs and margins
- **Run reports** — see total waste cost by period, breakdown by reason, and top 10 most-wasted items

### Who It's For
Independent restaurants, cafes, and bars with 1-3 locations doing $500K-$2M annual revenue. If you're on Square and you care about food cost, this is for you.

We built InventorySaaS to fill that gap — powerful enough to save you real money, simple enough to use during service, priced at what an independent restaurant can actually afford.
